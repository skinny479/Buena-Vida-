Founder & Chiropractor

Dr. Anthony Le, D.C.

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Le opened Buena Vida Chiropractic in Springdale in 2019 with a simple mission: help his community live life like they deserve by removing nerve interference and letting the body work at its best.

Chiropractic changed his own life early on, and he built Buena Vida to give that same experience to every patient. He takes time to listen, explains what's happening and why, and builds care plans around real-life goals.

A leader in the Northwest Arkansas business community, Dr. Le was named to Talk Business & Politics' Forty Under 40 list in 2026.
