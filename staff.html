Founder & Chiropractor

Dr. Anthony Le, D.C.

Doctor of Chiropractic · Hablo español

Dr. Le is the son of immigrants: a father from Vietnam and a mother, Guadalupe, from Mexico. He lost his father when he was two. His mother raised him and his sister while working several jobs, and he grew up immersed in Hispanic culture.

He was the first in his family to graduate from high school, then the first to graduate from college. Working alongside a chiropractor as a young man showed him what he wanted to do with his life. In 2019 he opened Buena Vida Chiropractic in Springdale.

He chose the name Buena Vida to welcome Northwest Arkansas's growing Hispanic community. Patients who are more comfortable in Spanish can talk with him directly about their care.

“I get my drive and work ethic from my mom.”

His philosophy is simple: keep moving your body the way it's meant to move. Today the clinic has served close to 4,000 patients. In 2026, Talk Business & Politics named Dr. Le to its Forty Under 40 list.

Our values

Family first. Community always.

Dr. Le remembers being the kid at school who sat alone on the days parents came to visit. He never wants his kids, or his employees' kids, to feel that way. That's why everyone here makes time for school drop-offs, recitals and games. It's a rule, not a perk.

As a minority-owned, bilingual practice, we know how much it matters to feel seen, heard and respected when you're looking for care. Everyone who walks through our door gets patience, honesty, and a team that's in their corner.

Live life naturally, optimally, and fully. Get back to living the Buena Vida — the good life.
