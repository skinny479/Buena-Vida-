Chiropractic Adjustments

Your nervous system controls every cell in your body. When the spine is out of alignment, it can interfere with those signals and cause pain, stiffness and reduced function. Adjustments restore proper motion and take that pressure off.

  • Full-spine adjustments for the neck, mid-back and lower back
  • Extremity adjustments for shoulders, hips, knees, wrists and ankles
  • Gentle, low-force options for sensitive patients
  • Care for every stage of life, from student athletes to seniors

  • Myofascial work to improve range of motion
