FAQ

Common questions

Does an adjustment hurt?

Most people find adjustments comfortable and relieving. You might hear a “pop,” which is just gas releasing from the joint. We also offer gentle, low-force techniques.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can schedule directly with us. Some insurance plans have their own referral rules, and we're happy to help you check.

Do you accept my insurance?

Yes. We accept all insurance. Our team will verify your benefits before your visit so you know what to expect.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on your condition, how long you've had it, and your goals. After your exam, we'll give you a clear recommendation, with no surprises and no pressure.

Do I have to be an athlete to get a sports massage?

Not at all. Sports massage helps anyone with tight muscles, a physical job, or a desk-bound routine, plus weekend warriors and serious competitors.

Can I combine services in one visit?

Yes. Many patients pair an adjustment with sports massage. Combining therapies often brings faster, longer-lasting results.

Do you treat auto accident injuries?

Yes. We evaluate and treat whiplash and other accident injuries, and we provide the documentation your claim needs.
