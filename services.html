Services

Whole-body care, built around you

Every plan starts with understanding you. Then we bring together the right mix of chiropractic, soft-tissue and holistic therapies to get you feeling and moving your best.

🦴

Chiropractic Adjustments

Your nervous system controls every cell in your body. When the spine is out of alignment, it can interfere with those signals and cause pain, stiffness and reduced function. Adjustments restore proper motion and take that pressure off.

  • Full-spine adjustments for the neck, mid-back and lower back
  • Extremity adjustments for shoulders, hips, knees, wrists and ankles
  • Gentle, low-force options for sensitive patients
  • Care for every stage of life, from student athletes to seniors

Sports Massage

Muscles hold tension, scar tissue and knots that can pull joints out of balance. Sports massage gets into the soft tissue to restore flexibility and speed up recovery. You don't have to be an athlete to benefit.

  • Pre-event and post-event recovery sessions
  • Deep tissue and trigger-point release
  • Myofascial work to improve range of motion
  • Pairs with adjustments so your results hold longer
RecoveryTight musclesInjury preventionPerformance

Shockwave Therapy

Shockwave therapy sends focused pulses of acoustic energy into injured or stubborn tissue. It boosts circulation and kick-starts your body's own repair process. It's non-invasive and drug-free, with no downtime, and works well for pain that just won't go away.

  • Plantar fasciitis and heel pain
  • Tendon problems: tennis and golfer's elbow, Achilles, patellar and shoulder tendons
  • Chronic muscle tightness and trigger points
  • Sports injuries that are slow to heal
  • Quick sessions, usually as a short series, paired with adjustments and rehab for best results

Corrective Exercise & Rehab

Adjustments create change, and exercise makes it stick. We teach you targeted stretches and strengthening work that supports your spine and keeps problems from coming back.

  • Customized home exercise programs
  • Core stability and postural strengthening
  • Mobility work for desk workers and active adults
  • Return-to-sport progressions
🚗

Auto Accident & Work Injuries

Whiplash and work injuries often don't show up right away. Early evaluation matters. We provide thorough exams, treatment and the documentation your case needs.

  • Whiplash and soft-tissue injury care
  • Detailed records and reports
  • Coordination with your attorney or insurer on request
🌿

Wellness & Lifestyle Coaching

Feeling better is the start. Staying well is the goal. We help you build habits that support a healthy, pain-free life.

  • Posture and workstation ergonomics
  • Nutrition and anti-inflammatory lifestyle guidance
  • Maintenance care plans to keep you at your best
